# The prompt

## Word for word
> <the request exactly as it was given>

## Answers to our questions
- **Who uses the output, and what do they do with it?**
- **What data do we have? Where, how much, how messy?**
- **What does done look like at the end?** (a demo, a number, a pull request)
- **Off limits?** (external calls, a required model, existing codebase vs standalone)
- **Which mistake costs more?** (the error trade-off, in their words)
